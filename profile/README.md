## Welcome to SparkHacks!

### What is SparkHacks?
SparkHacks is a 24-hour hackathon at the University of Illinois Chicago. It started in 2023 through the combined effort of multiple CS-based student organizations and has since grown into its own independent student organization. SparkHacks gives students an environment to learn, collaborate with peers, and build a project around our problem statements for a chance to win prizes.

### Why join SparkHacks?
**Experience:** get hands-on experience building something outside your standard curriculum. Add it as a side project on your resume to set yourself apart during your internship search.

**Technical Skills:** pre-hackathon and sponsor workshops give you a chance to explore CS from an industry perspective.

**Community:** surviving CS is about building your support network. Ideate and code with friends, eat free food, wear free merch, play games, and win prizes.
